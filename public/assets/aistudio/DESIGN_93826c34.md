## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an executive-grade operational command cockpit engineered specifically for 75-inch interactive interactive touch displays. The design aesthetic is clinical, composed, and architectural—rejecting consumer SaaS dashboard cliches such as purple gradients, frosted glass blur, floating decorative blobs, and hyper-dense spreadsheet tables.

The experience commands visual quietude at a standing distance of 1 to 3 meters. It serves executive steering committees, mission directors, and cross-functional enterprise project leads who require rapid visual ingestion without perceptual fatigue. Touch interactions prioritize zero-latency certainty: tactile tap targets, explicit hairline boundaries, and high-legibility typographic hierarchies that remain unambiguous under boardroom overhead lighting.

## Colors

The color system is calibrated for expansive surface clarity. The canvas relies on a cool architectural ground (`#F5F7FA`) anchored by an authoritative deep navy utility crown (`#112B42`). Cards and focal modules utilize crisp stark white (`#FFFFFF`) with precision hairline separations (`#DFE7EC`).

Semantic urgency uses an interpolated OKLCH spectrum to convey deadline proximity without inducing visual panic:
- **Baseline Calm:** Restrained teal (`#0B7D83`) indicates unthreatened delivery windows.
- **Early Warning (0–40% window):** Warm gold (`#C68A1B`) surfaces early caution.
- **Mid Warming (40–75% window):** Amber-orange (`#D96520`) signifies active compression.
- **Late Warning (75–100% window):** Signal red (`#D32F2F`) commands immediate remediation.
- **Critical Overdue:** Deep high-contrast crimson (`#941313`) paired with stark white typographic counter-strokes.
- **Structural States:** Static purple (`#7D2882`) marks blocked workflows, while quiet forest green (`#1B6D42`) marks verified completion.

## Typography

The type scale strictly enforces long-range ergonomics for large 75-inch physical viewports. Manrope establishes architectural presence across display banners, lane titles, and modal headers. Inter powers metadata, task descriptors, and numerical operations.

Typography sizing adheres to physical distance metrics:
- Small or dense typography below 14px is strictly banned across all layouts.
- Primary visual scans rely on 20px through 28px hierarchies for instant status comprehension 3 meters away.
- Numerical readouts use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jitter across continuous time and urgency recalculations.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an 8px rhythmic matrix. Operations panels use a structured horizontal orchestration pattern across 1920×1080 and 3840×2160 screen viewports:
- **Board Canvas Margins:** 32px (2rem) outer boundaries preserve interactive safety away from physical display bezels.
- **Panel Columns:** High-level project lanes operate with a 24px (1.5rem) column gutter, expanding across fixed multi-track columns.
- **Touch Heights:** Interactive elements mandate a strict physical touch envelope. Primary controls and drawer trigger buttons must maintain 56px to 64px physical heights, with secondary touch targets never dropping below 48px. Task operational rows scale between 72px and 96px in total vertical footprint to accommodate physical fingertip landing zones without risk of accidental adjacent triggering.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through architectural tonal tiers and razor-sharp structural outlines, deliberately avoiding heavy blur gradients or glassmorphism:
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Base operational ground `#F5F7FA`. Zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Shells):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px solid hairline (`#DFE7EC`). Grounded with an ambient, low-contrast shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(17, 43, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(17, 43, 66, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Lifted Touch Targets):** Tapped cards and reordered modules elevate with an immediate tactical displacement: `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(17, 43, 66, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(17, 43, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Command Drawers & Touch Dialogs):** Edge-docked contextual sheets and modal command panels float above an executive scrim (`rgba(17, 43, 66, 0.45)`): `box-shadow: -8px 0 32px rgba(17, 43, 66, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced visual geometry with dual-tier rounding:
- **Controls & Micro-Components:** Interactive controls, buttons, input fields, and status badges utilize a unified 12px radius, delivering finger-friendly ergonomics without appearing playful or child-like.
- **Panels & Structural Modules:** Column frames, category containers, floating cards, and sliding drawers utilize an 18px radius, providing polished containment that cleanly frames dense business metrics.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Operational Button:** Minimum height 56px (up to 64px for global actions). Background is `#0B7D83` with `#FFFFFF` text in Inter SemiBold 18px. Active press state triggers an immediate tonal shift to `#085F64` with a 0.98 scale transform within 120ms.
- **Secondary / Action Icon:** Height 48px to 56px. Background is `#FFFFFF`, hairline border 1px solid `#DFE7EC`, icon color `#14283E`.

### Cards & Task Rows
- **Card Containers:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, 18px corner radius, hairline 1px border `#DFE7EC`. Internal padding maintains a consistent 24px breathing room.
- **Task Row:** Uniform row height of 72px to 88px. Left edge features a 6px semantic accent bar that conveys dynamic urgency. Hover states are discarded in favor of explicit 150ms active touch-fill states (`rgba(11, 125, 131, 0.06)`).

### Badges & Urgency Indicators
- All status badges maintain a minimum height of 36px with 16px horizontal padding to ensure clear legibility from across the room.
- Every state pairs an explicit typographic label with a uniform Lucide stroke icon (e.g., AlertCircle, CheckCircle2, Octagon) to prevent reliance on color alone.

### Touch Dialogs & Command Drawers
- Drawers dock flush to the right or bottom screen boundary with full-height utility framing.
- Header bars utilize `#112B42` with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` controls and prominent 48px touch dismiss zones placed conveniently within arm's reach.
